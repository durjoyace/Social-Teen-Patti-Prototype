Provably Fair. Not a marketing claim.

Before every hand, we lock the deck with a cryptographic hash. After the hand, you verify it wasn't changed. Mathematics — not trust.

1

We Lock the Deck

Server generates a random seed and shuffles the deck. Creates a SHA-256 hash.

2

You See the Hash

The hash appears in your game header BEFORE any cards are dealt.

3

Game Plays Out

Cards are dealt from the locked deck. We can't change it — the hash would break.

4

You Verify

After the hand, tap "Verify." The hash must match. If it doesn't, we cheated. It always matches.
