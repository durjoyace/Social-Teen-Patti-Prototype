# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Adults aged 18 or older who want to play social Teen Patti at a private table with friends they already know. Their primary job is to create or join a friend table and start playing in under a minute.

## Product Purpose

Make a private Teen Patti game with friends quick to organize, trustworthy to play, and easy to return to. Success means an adult player can enter, create or join a table, invite a real friend, and begin a social game with minimal setup.

## Positioning

Private, server-authoritative Teen Patti rooms paired with verified social-play rewards. Club Points are earned through genuine multiplayer participation and unlock cosmetic extras; they are not a cash-like currency.

## Operating Context

The current priority is a board-facing web beta. Players enter through the web app, confirm that they are 18 or older, continue as a guest or account holder, create or join a private table, and share a link or room code with existing friends.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Web beta first.
- Purchases remain disabled.
- Club Points and play chips have no cash value and cannot be purchased, transferred, wagered, or cashed out.
- The core journey must preserve private-room creation and joining, friend invitations, server-authoritative play, reconnect behavior, and verified social-play rewards.
- Public-facing copy must use `Club Points`; legacy internal Beli identifiers are implementation details only.
- Do not fabricate testimonials, live activity, customer counts, performance claims, or other trust signals.

## Evidence on Hand

The repository contains a working React web application, server-backed private rooms, invite attribution, Club Points rewards, responsible-play documentation, production runbooks, automated verification, and a live web-beta deployment. No approved testimonials, customer logos, press coverage, or public usage claims are on hand; future design work must not invent them.

## Product Principles

1. Get friends to a private table with the least possible friction.
2. Make social play—not currency or artificial urgency—the center of the experience.
3. Earn trust through clear rules, server-authoritative behavior, and honest product states.
4. Keep every reward visibly separate from cash value or wagering.
5. Make the board demo representative of the real product rather than a scripted façade.
