Referral terms

The referral program is for eligible players aged 18 or older. A referral qualifies only when a genuinely new referred player completes their first verified multiplayer game with at least one other human.

Installation, account creation, practice or bot-only play, incomplete games, self-referrals, duplicate accounts, automated activity, and unverifiable activity do not qualify. We may delay or reject rewards where signals indicate duplicate devices, unusual velocity, manipulation, technical error, or a breach of these rules.

Beli is a promotional loyalty point for identity extras. It cannot be bought, transferred, wagered, converted to chips, exchanged for money, or cashed out.

Referral privacy

Referral attribution uses account IDs, referral code, campaign/source, timestamps, status, a qualifying game identifier, and risk flags. Device and IP signals are transformed with a keyed HMAC before storage; raw device IDs and IP addresses are not written to referral records.

These records are used to operate rewards, prevent abuse, resolve support issues, and maintain ledger integrity. Risk hashes remain pseudonymous personal data and are restricted to authorized operational use. Referral codes, risk hashes, email, phone, usernames, and device identifiers are not sent to product analytics.

You can permanently delete your account from Settings in the web or mobile app. Profile identifiers, social connections, notifications, and authored social content are removed or anonymized immediately. Pseudonymous gameplay, economy, payment, and referral ledger records are retained only for integrity, fraud prevention, and legal obligations under the final approved retention schedule.

The final public notice must state the approved retention schedule, subprocessors, deletion/access route, security contact, and India-specific lawful basis before launch.

Responsible play

Social Teen Patti is adults-only entertainment. The app records an 18+ confirmation for new accounts. Beli is non-purchasable, non-transferable, non-wagerable, and has no cash value. Referral rewards are cosmetic identity extras, never chips or prizes.

Purchases are disabled by default. Purchased play value must remain unavailable until legal, platform, state-availability, KYC/geofencing, refund, and stronger responsible-play controls are approved.
