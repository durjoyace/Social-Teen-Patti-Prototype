# Responsible play policy

Social Teen Patti is an adults-only entertainment product operated by Battle Green Consulting. The entry flow requires an 18+ confirmation. This confirmation is not a substitute for legal age assurance where stronger verification is required.

## Product boundaries

- Club Points are non-purchasable, non-transferable, non-wagerable, and have no cash value.
- Referral rewards are cosmetic/identity extras, not chips or prizes.
- AI/practice play never activates a referral.
- Purchases are disabled by default in every environment.
- Do not enable purchased play chips until Indian counsel, platform-policy review, age controls, state availability rules, refund handling, and any required KYC/geofencing are complete.

## Player protections required before a broad launch

- Clearly label practice, social, and any purchased-value modes.
- Provide session reminders, mute controls, block/report flows, account deletion, and an easy support route.
- Avoid countdown pressure, misleading “free” currency claims, loss-chasing copy, and cash-like rupee symbols on virtual balances.
- Publish odds/rules for every variant and preserve auditable server game records.
- Add configurable time-outs and self-exclusion before enabling monetized play.

Marketing must not target minors, imply guaranteed earnings, or describe Club Points or play chips as money.
