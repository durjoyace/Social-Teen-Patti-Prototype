# Referral terms

Last updated: 29 July 2026

The referral program is available to eligible players aged 18 or older. A referral becomes eligible only when a genuinely new referred player completes their first verified multiplayer game with at least one other human player.

Account creation, app installation, practice play, games played only with bots, incomplete games, self-referrals, duplicate accounts, automated activity, and activity that cannot be verified do not qualify.

When a referral qualifies, each player receives the Beli amount shown in the app. Milestone awards are based on verified activated referrals, not links sent or accounts created. Beli is a promotional loyalty point. It cannot be purchased, transferred, wagered, converted to chips, exchanged for money, or cashed out.

We may delay, reject, reverse, or review rewards where activity indicates duplicate devices, unusual velocity, manipulation, technical error, or a breach of these terms. Players should have a published support route to challenge an incorrect decision. Program amounts, extras, and availability may change prospectively; already redeemed entitlements will not be removed except for fraud or a legal requirement.

These terms must be reviewed by qualified Indian counsel and localized before public launch. They supplement, and do not replace, the main Terms of Service and Privacy Notice.
